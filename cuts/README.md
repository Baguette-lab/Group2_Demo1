# The selected lyric cuts

These thirteen WAV files are prepared from the group’s supplied recordings. They contain only the chosen passages. The separate moments in 2 MINUS 1 are joined in its cut, so playback cannot drift into the intervening lyrics.

The recordings stay in their original stereo sample rates. Playback begins at the start of a short PCM audio file; it does not depend on a browser seeking through a long MP3. Line clicks use the clip-relative timings in presentation-data.js. The full original MP3s remain in mp3/ for the separate playlist.

If the group changes a selected passage or replaces a recording, regenerate its cut and update its source times, clip times, and cueVersion together. A cue from a different recording or video version may not match.

True Love Waits uses the group’s six selected lines from the November 9 PDF and the uploaded 2016 studio recording. Its source span is 0:26.17–1:27.40. Starts were aligned to the matching version’s LRCLIB line cues (record 34645); the words remain as supplied by the group. The short cut retains the original vocal.
