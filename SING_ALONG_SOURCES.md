## Version details and exceptions

- **Vines:** No dedicated on-screen lyric video was verified. The [authorized Tower Sessions performance](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oF2AdW0XPeE) includes complete lyrics in its description. The site also links a [public text page licensed by LyricsFind](https://lyricsweb.com/song/the-ridleys/the-ridleys-vines). The supplied studio MP3 remains the local recording.
- **Hallelujah:** The official [Glastonbury lyric video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NcwF9lQx2us) is preferred over fan uploads. It is a live version and differs from the studio MP3, including possible verse differences. A studio-version fan ranking was incomplete, so no claim of highest views is made.
- **Water the Roses:** The [official production notes](https://flavorfoley.com/FFF/wtr) confirm lyrics rendered as fridge-magnet poetry. The artist’s [Bandcamp release](https://flavorfoley.bandcamp.com/album/water-the-roses) links the selected music video. The [track page](https://flavorfoley.bandcamp.com/track/water-the-roses) also provides text.
- **Silent Sanctuary’s Paalam:** The [band’s video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w_MxvOOK-nc) explicitly includes lyrics and was published October 21, 2012. The [current digital single](https://music.apple.com/us/album/paalam-single/1396377493) is dated June 29, 2018. The site uses 2012 as the earlier publication year and identifies the digital reissue in its reference. This metadata link is not used as a lyrics source.
- **Unti-Unti:** Viva Records’ explicitly titled Official Lyric Visuals is selected. The earlier [Globe campaign lyric video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qynqrst9ams), 35,341,890 indexed views, remains another authorized lyric source. Official availability takes priority over view count.

Embedded videos use youtube-nocookie.com. Each has a direct YouTube fallback. Publisher, country, age, network, and embedding restrictions may still affect availability. The selected-lines text stage contains only passages supplied by the group; this package does not copy full lyrics from third-party pages.

True Love Waits was added November 9, 2026, using the group’s uploaded recording and selected passage. Its published video is a fan lyric upload; no official on-screen lyric source was verified. The native story cut plays the selected words locally, with the original vocal.
