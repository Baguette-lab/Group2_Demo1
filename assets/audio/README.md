# Room for Two — story instrumental

An original, gentle electric-keys instrumental composed for Sa Iisang Bubong. It uses synthesized notes, soft chords, bass, and quiet echoes. No samples or existing song recordings were used. You may use, modify, and redistribute it with this project.

`story-ambience.mp3` is bundled locally and loops during dialogue and after a song-cut discussion. It starts after a story interaction, fades in, pauses for lyric clips, stops on other tabs, and pauses when the browser tab is hidden. Story music has its own on/off and volume controls. The separate full-song playlist and spliced lyric cuts are unchanged.

`compose-ambience.py` is the optional reproducible source. Rebuilding it needs Python, NumPy, and ffmpeg; running the website needs none of those tools.
