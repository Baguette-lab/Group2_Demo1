FULL SONGS AND STORY CUTS
Full playlist plays the complete MP3 recordings, with Play all, pause/resume, seeking, and automatic next-song playback. The spliced WAV clips, selected lyrics, and line highlighting live only in Story & song cuts. Switching between them clears the prior recording.

PIXEL SCENERY
The rooms, commute, and separate homes use the same eight-unit pixel grid as the characters. Readable labels and interface text remain separate from the low-resolution scenery.

STORY MUSIC
A gentle instrumental loop is included locally. Start the story or tap a dialogue/chapter control to hear it. Use Music on/off and Volume below the dialogue. It pauses for lyric clips and stays separate from the full-song playlist.
